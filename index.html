About

Bio

Estéfano Gómez is a mixed media artist based in Ecuador. His work treats the internet as a place we live in, one that now shapes how we experience culture and how we make it.

Interested in internet culture as one of the defining cultural shifts of our time, he investigates the connection between digital systems, art and everyday life. His current practice uses mixed media and interactive experiments, including hand tracking, gesture driven pieces and real time generative visuals.
